# January 2027 class fill — strategy

**Brand:** SKMS Krav Maga (Scot Krav Maga Ltd)  
**Site:** https://scotkravmaga.co.uk  
**Campaign window:** ~8 Nov 2026 → 7 Jan 2027 (women hard stop); teens/adults continue through January  
**Meta budget:** £30 per audience (£90 total)

## Goals

| Audience | New people | Offer | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teens (11–15) | ~15 | £10 trial → £40/mo; Sat 9–10am, Firhill Sports Complex | Book teens trial |
| Adults (Wed / Thu / Sat) | 10–20 total | £12 trial (£10 student) → £40 Kelvinhall / £55 unlimited | Book adult trial |
| Women-only beginners | ~20 | 4 weeks from **7 Jan 2027**, Thu 6–7pm, Glasgow Club Kelvinhall; £40 / £30 students | Book SumUp course |

Success = paid bookings / trials started, not just clicks.

## Funnel

```
Awareness (Meta ads, organic, events, GBP, referrals)
  → Landing (existing site pages or SumUp)
  → Trial (£10 / £12) or 4-week women course (£40 / £30)
  → Monthly membership (teens £40; adults £40–£55)
```

## Spend split (£30 each)

Hold ~£10 per audience for a final push.

| Audience | Weeks 1–6 | Final push | Stop when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Women-only | £20 (start Week 1) | £10 late Dec–6 Jan | ~20 booked |
| Teens | £20 (start Week 2) | £10 early Jan | ~15 new members |
| Adults | £20 (start Week 2) | £10 early Jan | 10–20 across three classes |

Meta alone will not hit these numbers at £30. Plan assumes **ads + organic + events + referrals + SEO** together.

## SEO policy (site)

**Allowed:** meta titles/descriptions, Open Graph, schema text, heading/body wording, alt text — anything that improves search without changing site structure.  
**Not allowed this campaign:** new routes, nav restructure, new pages, layout redesign.  
**Keyword map:** see `keywords.md`. Meta/hero wording pass applied on homepage, women, teens, adults, trial, timetable, classes, West End and Kelvinhall pages (Nov 2026). Kids class dropped — teens only.

## Channel mix

1. **Meta ads** — three separate campaigns; statics from `public/` / gallery; see `ads-meta.md`
2. **Organic IG + FB** — 3–4 posts/week total; rotate audiences; see `organic-social.md`
3. **Events listings** — Facebook Events, Eventbrite (free), local What’s On / uni boards; see `events-listings.md`
4. **Google unpaid** — GBP posts, review asks, plus on-site meta/wording SEO (no structure change); see `google-gbp.md` + `keywords.md`
5. **Member referral** — one WhatsApp/email blast; see `outreach.md`
