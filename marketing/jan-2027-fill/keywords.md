## Do / don’t

**Do**
- Prefer **self defence** (UK) in owned copy  
- Put the main keyword in the **first 100 characters** of titles, GBP posts, and event titles  
- Lead with benefits people understand (confidence, beginners welcome, women-only, venues) — not acronyms

**Don’t**
- Stuff every synonym into one title  
- Create new URLs just for keywords  
- Put **IKMF** in heroes, ads, or primary SEO copy (most visitors won’t know it; fine on instructor credential pages only)  
- Rank-chase “martial arts Glasgow” as primary (too broad / competitive for £0 SEO this cycle)
